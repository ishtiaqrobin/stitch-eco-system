1. Introduction

Welcome to UniMart, an editorial social commerce platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our marketplace, social features, and mobile applications.

By using UniMart, you agree to the practices described in this policy. We reserve the right to modify this document as our services evolve, and we will notify you of any significant changes via the email associated with your account.

data_usage

2. Information We Collect

Direct Information

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment details during checkout.

Social Interaction

Curated product reviews, social posts, comments, and followers/following data within the UniMart community ecosystem.

Automated & Technical Data

Device identifiers, IP addresses, browsing patterns, and cookie-based preferences to personalize your editorial feed.

3. How We Use Data

  • check_circle
    Transaction Processing

    Fulfilling orders, processing payments, and managing delivery logistics across Bangladesh and international hubs.

  • check_circle
    Personalized Editorial Experience

    Using machine learning to curate your social feed, showing products and stories that align with your aesthetic preferences.

  • check_circle
    Communication

    Sending order updates, promotional newsletters (with opt-out), and critical security alerts regarding your account.

4. Data Security

We employ bank-level encryption and rigorous internal protocols to ensure your data remains confidential. All financial transactions are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share data with trusted partners necessary for business operations: delivery partners (e.g., Pathao, RedX), payment processors, and cloud infrastructure providers. All partners are contractually bound to maintain your privacy.

6. Your Rights

As a UniMart user, you have full control over your data. You may exercise the following rights at any time via your account settings:

visibility Right to Access
edit Right to Rectification
delete Right to Erasure
download Data Portability

Have Questions About Your Privacy?

Our Data Protection Officer is available to clarify any concerns regarding your information and our security practices.
