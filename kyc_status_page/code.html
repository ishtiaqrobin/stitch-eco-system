Frequently Asked Questions

How long does verification take?

Typically, documents are reviewed within 24-48 business hours. You will receive a notification once the status changes.

Can I start selling before verification?

Yes, you can list products and receive orders, but fund withdrawals will be restricted until KYC is completed.
